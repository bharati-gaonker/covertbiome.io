# CovertBiome Website

Source for [covertbiome.io](https://covertbiome.io/).

CovertBiome is exploring AI-driven computational biology approaches for decoding hidden biological signals from fragmented biomedical data.

## Public research

The public technical research repository is available at:

**https://github.com/bharati-gaonker/covertbiome-research**

The public research repository includes research architecture, reproducible configuration, baseline multimodal research code, data-harmonization scaffolding, tests, CI, and documentation. The initial public proof of concept focuses on pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).

Proprietary training recipes, causal/mechanistic reasoning systems, private model weights, partner data, and unpublished therapeutic hypotheses remain outside the public repository.

## Research use

CovertBiome is an early-stage research effort. Public materials are for research and hypothesis generation and are not intended for diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or clinical decision support.
